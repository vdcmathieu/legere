# Legere audit - 2026-10-07

Independent adversarial audit of the matching pipeline (code, data, codings) of https://legere.vandecatsije.com.
Repo state: `main` at 01dc8e1. No repo file was modified. All scripts and outputs are in this directory.

## Executive summary

**Is the tool currently matching users to the right candidates?** Mostly yes, and the mechanics are sound.
The answer scale, the build transform, the statement-id alignment, the retest reversal formula and the three gate imputations are all correct (no sign flip anywhere).
Every one of the 12 candidates' perfect clones ranks first, both with all 80 answers and through the real flow (10 core + adaptive + stop rule, 20 seeds, median 20 answers), even with 25% response jitter (Faure's clone slips to Tondelier in 1 of 20 noisy runs).

**Where it is wrong.** The problem is in the codings, and it is concentrated in two places:

1. **Core statements shown to every user carry asymmetric or wrong cells.** Of the 10 core items, 6 have a confirmed defect: ret1 (Faure understated at +1 while he campaigns on 62), ecol2 (Attal null despite two personal roll-call votes for re-authorising acetamiprid; Roussel +1 inferred while his group voted 17-0 and 15-0 the other way), eco2 (Le Pen -1 for the same vote that gives Attal and Philippe -2, with a direct "stupide et nocive" quote available), int3 (Retailleau's -2 is "inferred low" although he wrote "sortir de l'OTAN ... ce serait un cadeau"; Glucksmann/Faure stuck at -1 with the same evidence quality as Attal's -2), eu4 (Attal +1 against the cell's own evidence), imm3 (Faure null although the PS project says "Défendre l'AME").
2. **Faure is under-coded** (47 cells, 23 of them unsourced "low" party-line codes) although the PS project site the coder already used states positions on six of his null cells. His clone has the thinnest margin in the whole matrix (0.045 over Tondelier).

**How many cells are wrong?** 20 confirmed findings covering 19 cells (3 high, 9 medium, 8 low): 11 null cells that should be coded, 5 magnitude errors, 1 sign error (Le Pen trav3 +1 -> 0), 3 evidence/confidence upgrades that change weights on core items. A further 13 questionable findings (neutrality asymmetries, one wording issue, one unverified aggregator claim on Zemmour's retirement age) need a maintainer decision.
Across the 733 coded cells, no cell was found whose sign contradicts its own quoted evidence except Attal eu4 (and arguably Le Pen trav3).

**Does it change who wins?** Not for a committed voter: after applying the confirmed corrections (and after also applying the questionable ones), all 12 clones still win 10/10 noisy runs.
For respondents "between" two candidates (66 synthetic blends, 10 noisy runs each) the top-1 changes for 10 of 66 blends under the confirmed fixes, concentrated where expected: the Faure/Glucksmann/Tondelier triangle (glucksmann+tondelier: Tondelier 6 -> Faure 7), Attal versus Glucksmann (attal+glucksmann 7 -> 10 Glucksmann once Attal's ecol2 is coded), and Retailleau versus the Tondelier/Roussel/Ruffin side. Most of these blends were already unstable (split 5/5 or 6/4) under the current codings, so the realistic effect is a reshuffle inside already-close results, not a wholesale re-ranking.

**One context fact to track.** Polls now test Marine Le Pen (eligible again since the 7 July 2026 appeal ruling): Ifop-Fiducial, September 2026: Le Pen 31-36%, Mélenchon and Philippe 16-17%, Attal and Glucksmann 10-11%, Retailleau about 8%. `data/meta.json` has no `polls` key, so the "viabilité" hint for strategic voters is empty. The Toluna-Harris poll of 18-19 Aug 2026 also tests François Hollande as a PS-primary hypothesis; he is not in the candidate list (fine until the primary result on 17 Oct).

## Wording (FR vs EN)

All 80 `fr` statements were compared with `en`. The French is generally more precise than the English (explanatory clauses added) without changing scope, and the codings follow the French. Items worth knowing:
- **soc4** is a negated proposition (F26). Codings are consistent with the negation.
- **eco1** FR adds "qui taxe aussi les placements financiers, et pas seulement l'immobilier"; Le Pen's IFF (financial only, no real estate) is correctly +1 rather than +2.
- **ret2** "au-delà de 64 ans" while the legal age is frozen at 62 y 9 m: Attal's "abolish the legal age" is coded -1, defensible.
- **soc1** "Maintenir la loi de 2026": coder rule "-2 needs a repeal pledge" applied consistently (Le Pen, Retailleau, Philippe -1; Zemmour -2 with an explicit pledge).
- **trav3**, **edu3**, **ecol3** describe the 2026 legal state accurately (`plain_fr` checked: franchises cap 70+70 EUR from 1 Oct 2026 is correct per Vidal).
- No statement was found double-barrelled in a way that produced contradictory codings, with the exception of int6 ("militaire ou civique") where Mélenchon's compulsory civic service is rightly +2.
